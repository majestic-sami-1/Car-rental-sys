Database Schema \& Data Dictionary

Table Entities and Relationships

• Admin: Stores administrative staff credentials and permissions.

• Customer: Contains personal customer profiles and verified driver license numbers.

• Car: Inventory details including vehicle make, model, category, rate, and current availability status.

• Booking: Maps customer reservations to assigned cars with start/end schedules and calculated costs.

• Payment: Captures completed transaction records, amounts, payment timestamps, and methods.
